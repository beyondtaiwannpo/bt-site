一起做這件事。

我們持續在找願意一起做這件事的個人、企業與學校。合作不一定是錢，很多時候是一個場地、一個下午、或是一句「來我們學校講」。

合作過誰

美國在臺協會 AIT、EducationUSA、TRÈS BON 好伴 café、臺北市立第一女子高級中學。

這幾個單位幫過的事都不一樣：有的提供場地，有的把我們介紹給對的人，有的直接讓我們進校園。五年下來累積了什麼寫在另一頁。

可以怎麼合作

  • 場地一場探索營是兩天，需要一個能坐下幾十個高中生的空間。
  • 講者你如果走過海外升學這條路，回來講一個下午。對現場的高中生來說，那是一個真的存在的樣本，比任何簡報都有用。
  • 學校把 BT 帶進你的學校，讓學生在校內就問得到。
  • 贊助直接支持我們把活動辦下去。

贊助的錢用在哪

三件事：日常營運、暑期探索營、導生配對計畫。

學員不收費是刻意的設計，不是還沒開始收。我們處理的是資訊落差，把資訊放在付費牆後面就變成我們原本要解決的那個問題。所以這些活動能不能辦下去，靠的是願意支持的人。

怎麼開始

寫一封信到 beyondtaiwan2020@gmail.com，講一下你是誰、想怎麼參與。不用寫得很正式。

我們是學生組織，幹部都在上學，回信可能不會太快，但每一封都會看。

Build this with us.

We are always looking for individuals, companies and schools willing to build this with us. It does not have to be money. Often it is a room, an afternoon, or an invitation to come and speak.

Who we have worked with

The American Institute in Taiwan, EducationUSA, TRÈS BON café, and Taipei First Girls' High School.

Each of them helped in a different way: a venue, an introduction to the right people, a door into a school. What five years added up to is on another page.

Ways to work with us

  • A venueCollege Bootcamp runs for two days and needs a room that seats a few dozen teenagers.
  • SpeakingIf you have walked this path, come and spend an afternoon. To the students in the room you are a real example, which beats any slide deck.
  • Your schoolBring Beyond Taiwan into your school so students can ask their questions on campus.
  • SponsorshipSupport us so the programmes keep running.

Where sponsorship goes

Three things: day to day operations, the summer College Bootcamp, and the mentorship programme.

Students pay nothing, and that is deliberate. We work on an information gap, and putting information behind a paywall would recreate the very problem we set out to solve. So whether these programmes keep running depends on people willing to support them.

How to start

Write to beyondtaiwan2020@gmail.com and tell us who you are and how you would like to be involved. It does not need to be formal.

We are a student organisation and everyone is in school, so a reply may take a while, but we read all of them.
