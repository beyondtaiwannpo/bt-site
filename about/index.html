We believe that everyone has the right to choose their own education.

我們在處理的是資訊落差。

在台灣，「出國念書」常被當成一件家裡有錢才能想的事。

The gap we work on is information.

In Taiwan, studying abroad is still widely treated as something only well-off families get to consider.

這不完全是錯覺。留學代辦的費用不低，公立學校能給的海外升學資源，通常也比不上私立或國際學校。真正的問題不是錢而已，是資訊：你身邊如果沒有人走過，你連要問什麼都不知道。

That impression is not baseless. Agency fees are high, and public schools rarely have the staff or resources to guide a student through an overseas application. But the harder barrier is information. If no one around you has done it, you do not even know what to ask.

於是很多人在還沒認真了解之前，就先把這個選項刪掉了。不是因為不適合，是因為沒有人告訴他這件事實際上長什麼樣子。

So students rule the option out before they ever look into it. Not because it does not suit them, but because no one has shown them what it actually involves.

Beyond Taiwan 處理的就是這一段落差。我們幾乎所有活動都免費，導生配對計畫、探索營、講座，都不收費。我們沒辦法幫你做決定，但可以讓你在做決定之前，先知道自己有哪些選擇。

That gap is the reason Beyond Taiwan exists. Almost everything we run is free: mentorship, bootcamps, talks. We cannot make anyone's decision. We can make sure they know what they are choosing between.

「我們不是老師，也不是顧問。多數人只比你早走一兩步。」

"We are not teachers and we are not consultants. Most of us are only a step or two ahead of you."

使命

因此，我們為台灣公立學校的學生打開被忽略的可能，讓他們看見「唯一一條路」之外的選項。那可能是走向世界去留學，也可能是留在台灣，用更開闊的方式學習與生活。

Mission

Therefore, we help students in Taiwan's public schools discover the possibilities they were never shown: the options beyond the single path they'd been told to follow. That might mean studying abroad, or staying in Taiwan and learning to live in a more open way.

我們同時維繫一個由「選擇了自己道路的人」所組成的社群，讓走過的人成為後來者的引路人，也讓這個網絡長久地延續下去。

And we're building a lasting community of people who chose their own path, where those who went before can guide the ones coming after.

願景

一個台灣，升學不再只有一條路。每個學生，無論來自哪裡，都能看見自己的選項，並擁有選擇的權利與勇氣。

Vision

A Taiwan where success is no longer defined by a single path, where every student, wherever they come from, can see their options and has the right, and the courage, to choose.

價值

  • 看見你無法選擇你從未看見的東西。
  • 平等選擇的權利，不該取決於出身或家庭的資源。
  • 陪伴沒有人該獨自摸索。走過的人，拉下一個人一把。

Values

  • SeeingYou can't choose what you've never seen.
  • EqualityThe right to choose shouldn't depend on where you come from or what your family has.
  • CompanyNo one should have to figure it out alone. Those who've walked the road pull the next person up.

Free on purpose

Students pay nothing. This is not a fee we have yet to introduce. It is a deliberate design choice.

The gap we work on is information. Putting information behind a paywall would recreate the very problem we exist to solve.
