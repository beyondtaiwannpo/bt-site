為什麼要登入Why sign in

東西是免費的，但我們想知道拿的人是誰、在哪一間學校。那決定我們明年把營隊辦在哪裡、辦給誰。開帳號兩分鐘，不用付錢，也不會有人打電話給你。The material is free, but we want to know who is taking it and which school they are at. That is what decides where we run next year's camp and for whom. An account takes two minutes, costs nothing, and nobody will call you.

我們保存哪些資料、誰看得到，寫在隱私政策裡。你隨時可以自己把帳號刪掉。What we keep and who can see it is in the privacy policy. You can delete your account yourself at any time.
