公開的團隊頁

幹部的姓名、團隊與大頭照預設不公開。要出現在網站的公開團隊頁上，需要兩件事同時成立：本人自己在帳號頁勾選同意，以及 Co-President 核可。任一方隨時可以撤回。

請注意：公開的網頁會被搜尋引擎收錄與封存。姓名撤下之後不會再顯示，但已被第三方封存的副本我們無法移除。未滿 18 歲的成員不會被放上公開頁面。

管理者的存取範圍

管理者透過資料庫後台，技術上能夠存取所有資料，包含心得與照片。任何需要維護資料庫的組織都無法在技術上排除這一點，因此我們以明確的內部規範限制它：

  • 不會為了任何非必要的理由讀取使用者的心得或照片
  • 收到與隱私有關的疑慮回報時，不會以「查證」為由讀取相關內容
  • 僅在使用者本人請求協助時（例如要求匯出或刪除資料）存取其資料

我們選擇明確說明這項限制，而非以概括的措辭迴避它。

你的瀏覽器裡存了什麼

登入後，我們會在你的瀏覽器存一筆登入憑證，用途只是讓你不必每次重新整理都重新登入。登出即清除。

我們不使用 cookie，也不追蹤你在其他網站的行為。

未成年使用者

BT 的成員包含未滿 18 歲者。我們採取以下措施：

  • 不公開任何使用者的個人聯絡方式，email 不會出現在其他使用者可見的任何位置
  • 不安排未經團隊記錄的一對一私下聯繫
  • 大頭照將顯示於進度牆供其他幹部查看，此事會在上傳當下再次提示
  • 使用者可隨時移除自己的大頭照

申請活動時，若你未滿 18 歲，申請表上會請你填寫家長或監護人的 email。送出之後，我們會寄一封信告知對方你申請了什麼活動、我們保存哪些資料。那是一封告知信，不需要回覆或簽名，也不會因為沒有填而擋下你的申請。

未滿 18 歲使用者的家長或監護人如需了解我們所儲存的資料，可來信洽詢。

你的權利

查看與更正：登入後可於護照中檢視全部資料，姓名、團隊與大頭照均可自行修改。

刪除帳號：學員可於帳號頁自行刪除，該操作會一併刪除你送出過的每一份申請與其中的答案。幹部請來信至 beyondtaiwan2020@gmail.com。

取消訂閱：於帳號頁取消勾選，或點擊任何一封通知信中的取消訂閱連結。

所有刪除操作皆為永久刪除，而非隱藏或停用，且無法復原。建議於刪除前先行匯出備份。

  • 不追蹤使用者在其他網站的行為

資料保存期間

我們目前沒有設定自動刪除的期限。你的資料，包含你送出過的申請與其中的答案，會保存到你自己刪除，或是你來信要求我們刪除為止。

我們選擇把這件事寫清楚，而不是寫成「保存至必要期間」——那句話在實務上沒有意義。相對地，刪除的方式一定是你自己按得到的：學員可於帳號頁自行刪除帳號與全部資料。

政策變更

若我們變更資料的收集或使用方式，將更新本頁日期並於網站公告。涉及新增資料類型或改變可見範圍等重大變更，將另行以 email 通知使用者。

About this policy

When you use our services at beyondtaiwannpo.com, we store certain information about you. This policy explains what we collect, who can access it, and how you can manage it.

About us

Beyond Taiwan is a non-profit organisation in Taiwan working to help students at public high schools see their options for studying abroad.

Contact: beyondtaiwan2020@gmail.com

The isolation of entries and photos is enforced by database-level access rules, not by hiding them in the interface. Other users cannot retrieve this content even by querying the system directly. Application content works the same way: an organiser outside the team that runs that programme cannot read it, even by querying the system directly.

The public team page

An organiser's name, team and photo are not public by default. Appearing on the site's public team page requires two things at once: the person agreeing on their own account page, and a Co-President approving it. Either side can withdraw at any time.

Please note: public pages are indexed and archived by search engines. A name removed from the page stops being displayed, but we cannot remove copies already archived by third parties. Members under 18 are not put on public pages.

Administrator access

Administrators can technically access all data, including entries and photos, through the database console. No organisation that maintains a database can eliminate this technically, so we constrain it by explicit internal rule:

  • We do not read anyone's entries or photos without a necessary reason
  • When a privacy concern is reported, we do not read the content in question in order to verify it
  • We access a user's data only when that user asks us for help, such as with an export or deletion request

We state this limitation explicitly rather than obscuring it with broader language.

What is stored in your browser

After you sign in, we store one login credential in your browser. Its only purpose is so that you do not have to sign in again every time the page reloads. It is cleared when you sign out.

We do not use cookies, and we do not track your activity on other websites.

Users under 18

Some BT members are under 18. We apply the following measures:

  • No user's personal contact details are made public; email addresses do not appear anywhere visible to other users
  • We do not arrange one-to-one private contact that is not recorded by the team
  • Profile photos are shown to other cadres on the progress wall; this is stated again at the moment of upload
  • Users may remove their profile photo at any time

When you apply to a programme, if you are under 18 the form asks for a parent or guardian email. After you submit, we send them one message saying what you applied for and what we keep. It is a notice, not a consent form; no reply or signature is needed, and leaving the field blank does not block your application.

Parents or guardians of users under 18 may contact us to learn what data we hold.

Your rights

Access and correction. All your data is visible in your passport once you sign in. Name, team, and profile photo can be changed by you at any time.

Export. The passport's details page provides an export function that downloads a file containing all of your data.

Deleting part of your data. The reset function permanently deletes your stamps, entries, and photos. This cannot be undone.

Deleting your account. Students can do this themselves on their account page; it also deletes every application they have submitted and the answers in it. Organisers should write to beyondtaiwan2020@gmail.com.

Unsubscribing. Untick the box on your account page, or use the unsubscribe link in any announcement.

All deletions are permanent rather than hidden or deactivated, and cannot be reversed. We recommend exporting a backup first.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade user data
  • We do not use user data for advertising
  • We do not use anyone's name, photo, or written content in external communications without consent
  • We do not track users across other websites

Retention

We currently set no automatic deletion period. Your data, including any applications you have submitted and the answers in them, is kept until you delete it yourself or ask us to delete it.

We say this plainly rather than writing "kept for as long as necessary", which means nothing in practice. In exchange, deletion is always something you can do yourself: students can delete their account and all of their data from their account page.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we collect or use data, we will update the date on this page and post a notice on the site. For significant changes, such as collecting a new category of data or changing who can see what, we will notify users by email.
