Thirty people. All students.

Two Co-Presidents and six teams. Our organisers are in Taiwan, the United States, Canada, Europe, Australia, Japan and Korea.

How it works

The two Co-Presidents lead three teams each.

Every team has a Director and one or two Advisors, who are the point of contact for anything crossing teams or going outside the organisation.

Everyone here is a student. Most are at university; some are still in high school.

Six teams

  • SponsorshipPartnerships and funding.

  • MentorshipMatching and mentoring.

  • CRCampus relations.

A new group every year

Beyond Taiwan hands over to a new group of organisers each year. That single fact shapes how we build every tool: keep it simple enough that the next person can read it and change it.

This website is built on that principle.
