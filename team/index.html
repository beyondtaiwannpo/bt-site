團隊

Curriculum

探索營 College Bootcamp

每年寒暑假各一場，兩天，免費。

兩天裡，你會看到學長姐當年真正送出去的申請文書，動手改一次自己的，再跟五、六位不同科系的人聊他們在國外的實際生活。

我們會事先問你想讀什麼，再去找對應背景的人回來。所以每一屆的講者名單都不一樣。

第一屆在 2023 年六月，超過 80 位高中生參加。

講座

不定期舉辦，也不收費。場次同樣會在 Instagram 公布。

Mentorship

導生配對計畫 Mentorship Program

一學期配對一次，免費。

你會有一位走過同一條路的學長姐。他不會幫你寫 essay，但會陪你把卡住的地方講清楚。

有些配對從申請季開始，到現在超過兩年還在聯絡。

導生制度從 2020 年九月開始，第一批 36 人。

Internship

高中生實習計劃

每學期收一批，從官網報名。

讓公立高中生在申請之前就有一段真的做過的事。實習生進 BT 的各個 team 做真的事，有自己的實習護照記錄做過什麼。

Community Relations

安排學長姐回公立高中演講，讓資訊到達本來到不了的地方；校園大使把消息帶進自己的學校。2021 年十月第一批校園大使 18 位。

Sponsorship

找願意一起做這件事的個人、企業與學校。嘖嘖募資也在這裡：2027 年 3 月 23 日上線，4 月 21 日結束。

Marketing

Instagram、大學介紹系列、官網的照片與影片。活動公告以 Instagram 為第一管道。

Leadership

我們不是老師，也不是顧問。多數人只比你早走一兩步，有些人剛申請完不久，還記得那種不知道從哪裡開始的感覺。

每年換一批人

BT 的幹部每年換屆。這件事決定了我們怎麼做每一個工具：能簡單就簡單，讓下一個接手的人看得懂、改得動。

這個網站本身就是照這個原則做的。

Teams

Curriculum

College Bootcamp

Twice a year, winter and summer. Two days. Free.

Over two days you read the essays alumni actually submitted, rewrite one of your own, and talk with five or six people studying very different subjects about what their lives abroad are really like.

We ask what you want to study before the camp, then bring back people with that background. No two years have the same line-up.

The first bootcamp ran in June 2023 with more than 80 high school students.

Talks

Held through the year, also free. Dates are announced on Instagram.

Mentorship

Mentorship Program

One matching round per semester. Free.

You are paired with someone who has been down the same road. They will not write your essay. They will sit with you until you can say out loud what you are stuck on.

Some pairs that started in an application season are still in touch more than two years later.

Mentorship began in September 2020 with 36 students.

Internship

High school internship

One intake a semester, apply on the site.

The high school internship programme gives public school students real work experience before they apply. Interns join BT's teams and do real work, tracked in their own intern passport.

Community Relations

Arranges alumni talks at public high schools so the information reaches places it never did; campus ambassadors carry the news into their own schools. The first 18 ambassadors started in October 2021.

Sponsorship

Finds the people, companies and schools willing to build this with us. The Zeczec crowdfunding campaign lives here too: live 23 March 2027, closes 21 April 2027.

Marketing

Instagram, the college introduction series, the photos and video on this site. Instagram is the first channel for every announcement.

Leadership

We are not teachers and we are not consultants. Most of us are only a step or two ahead of you. Some finished their own applications recently enough to still remember not knowing where to start.

A new group every year

Beyond Taiwan hands over to a new group of organisers each year. That single fact shapes how we build every tool: keep it simple enough that the next person can read it and change it.

This website is built on that principle.
