We believe that everyone has the right to choose their own education.

Beyond Taiwan is a group of students who came out of Taiwan's public high schools. We went looking, and we came back to tell you what we found.

活動與計畫Programmes

01

探索營College Bootcamp

每年寒暑假各一場，兩天。看學長姐當年真正送出去的申請文書，再跟五、六位不同科系的人聊他們在國外的實際生活。Twice a year, winter and summer, over two days. Read the essays alumni actually submitted, and talk with five or six people about what their lives abroad are really like.

→ 02

導生配對計畫Mentorship Program

一學期配對一次。你會有一位走過同一條路的學長姐，他不會幫你寫 essay，但會陪你把卡住的地方講清楚。One matching round per semester. You are paired with someone who has been down the same road. They will not write your essay. They will sit with you until you can say out loud what you are stuck on.

→
03

免費資源Free resources

申請文書怎麼寫、選校怎麼看、時程怎麼排。這些是 BT 五年下來一次一次講給學生聽的東西，寫下來讓更多人拿得到。How to write your essays, how to read a school list, how to plan the timeline. Five years of things we have explained one student at a time, written down so more people can have them.
