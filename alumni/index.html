他們從這裡出發。然後回來。

島上每一個亮點，是一所公立高中。點一個，看他飛去了哪裡。飛過的落點會留在圖上，點它可以反過來看那個人從台灣哪裡出發。

They left from here. Then they came back.

Every light on the island is a public high school. Tap one to see where that person flew. Places you have visited stay on the map, and tapping one shows where in Taiwan they started.

    點島上的任一個亮點。
    那個人會從他的高中飛到現在所在的城市，這裡會出現他是誰、去了哪個國家，還有他自己的一句話。    Tap any light on the island.
    That person flies from their high school to the city they live in now, and this is where you will see who they are, which country they went to, and something they said themselves.
